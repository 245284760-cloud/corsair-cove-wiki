## Find the Building Connection Node

- Select the building and locate its green connection arrow.
- Automatic connection can fail on steep terrain, so inspect the node instead of assuming a nearby route is connected.

## Choose the Correct Connection Piece

- Use a Road for ordinary ground.
- Use a Rope Bridge for water or gaps.
- Use a Cliff Path or Ladder for vertical cliff faces.

## Wait for Construction to Finish

An unconnected building cannot begin construction or operation. Finish the required connection before treating materials or labor as the problem.

## Diagnose a Slow Route

Green, yellow, and red route colors indicate transport efficiency. A yellow or red route can be complete but inefficient; a broken route has no usable connection to the building node.
