## Find the Building Connection Node

- Green connection arrows belong only to certain key connection hubs; some buildings do not require a connection.
- Select an eligible hub and locate its green connection arrow. Automatic connection can fail on steep terrain, so inspect the node instead of assuming a nearby route is connected.

## Choose the Correct Connection Piece

- Use a Road for ordinary ground.
- Use a Rope Bridge for water or gaps.
- Use a Cliff Path or Ladder for vertical cliff faces.

## Wait for Construction to Finish

An eligible connection hub cannot begin construction or operation until its required route is complete. Finish that connection before treating materials or labor as the problem; buildings without a connection requirement are the exception.

## Diagnose a Slow Route

Green, yellow, and red route colors indicate transport efficiency. A yellow or red route can be complete but inefficient; a broken route has no usable connection to an eligible hub node.
