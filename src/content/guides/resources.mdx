## Resource Categories

The official resource guide separates goods into construction, upkeep, and intermediate resources. Use the category to decide whether a shortage blocks building, continuously drains pirate or ship upkeep, or feeds another production step.

## Construction Resources

Construction resources are used for buildings and ships. Keep a reserve of Planks and other construction goods before expanding, because a new building or ship can consume stock faster than a single producer replaces it.

## Upkeep Resources

Upkeep resources are consumed by pirates and ship crews. Watch the island's net production rather than only the amount in storage: a positive balance can grow a reserve, while a negative balance will eventually stop meeting upkeep needs.

## Intermediate Resources

Intermediate resources sit between a source and a final consumer. Hardwood, for example, can feed both construction and upkeep chains. Treat an intermediate shortage as a chain problem and inspect its upstream producer before adding downstream buildings.

## How to Read a Resource Chain

Open the production overview to see the producers, inputs, outputs, and required deposits or fertile areas for a chain. The overview is the source of truth for the current chain shown by the game; do not infer a recipe from a similarly named resource.

## Stockpile and Net Production Checks

Before expanding, check storage space, current net production, worker availability, and whether a source and consumer are connected. If the net number is negative, solve the upstream shortage or reduce consumption before committing to another downstream building.
