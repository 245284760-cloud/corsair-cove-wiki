## How to Verify Future Announcements

Check the publisher or developer announcement first, then the relevant first-party store listing. Confirm that the listing names the platform and identifies whether it is a native build, cloud access, or a subscription entitlement. Update this page's verification date and status table only when that evidence exists; do not turn rumors or an absent listing into a confirmed release.
