## Verified Rope Maker Facts

The official Buildings table lists Rope Maker in the Ships category. It uses 2 Greenhands, takes Fibre as its input, costs 25 Planks to build, and the Resources table lists output of 4 Rope per minute from 8 Fabric.

## Fibre Input Boundary

The official pages confirm the Rope Maker input and the resource table's production values. They do not, in the checked excerpts, establish a complete placement route for every Fibre source, so do not treat this page as a map or a universal acquisition walkthrough.

## Production and Transport Checks

Before diagnosing a Rope shortage, check that the Rope Maker is connected, staffed, supplied with its input, and has output storage room. Then inspect the Fetcher route and upstream Fibre production using the production-chain checks.

## What Is Not Published

No unverified claim is made here about a fastest Fibre location, hidden unlock, or exact island layout. Use the official Buildings and Resources tables as the source of truth for the current version.
